Sort toys to the beat

A calm music and sorting game for ages 3 to 6

Toys ride along the conveyor belt in a warm wooden workshop. Your child drags each one into the right box, and every right answer rings a chime that lands on the beat. At the end, Milo the monkey and Pip the penguin dance while "the song you made" plays back.

Coming soon to Google Play

Made for little hands and big feelings

No failure, ever

No game over, no timers to beat, no failure sounds. A toy in the wrong box floats gently back to the belt.

Never off the beat

A right answer always counts. The music waits for your child, so every chime sounds like part of the song.

Easy for everyone

Big touch targets, one-finger dragging and no reading. Every colour also has a shape (red ●, blue ■, yellow ★), so colour-blind children can play too.

What children practise

  • Sorting by colour, size, shape and meaning (fruit or vegetable)
  • Spotting and continuing a simple pattern
  • Feeling a steady beat
  • Careful finger control

12 levels in 4 worlds, all open from the start. English and Urdu voice.

A look inside

For parents

  • Private by design: no accounts, ads, purchases, analytics or tracking. The app has no internet permission, so nothing leaves the device. Privacy Policy
  • Healthy screen time: choose a session length, and the app ends with a gentle "goodnight" scene after the current level.
  • Calm mode: softer colours, a slower belt and quieter music for children with sensory sensitivity.
  • Grown-up settings sit behind a simple maths check.
