Delete My Data

Rhythm Workshop, published by HomiLabs Solutions

We hold no data about you or your child. There is no account and nothing is stored on our servers.

The only information the app keeps is on your own device: the stars your child has earned and the parent settings. You can delete it yourself in any of these ways.

Option 1: Reset progress in the app

  1. On the Home screen, press and hold the small grey gear in the top-right corner.
  2. Answer the grown-up question.
  3. Scroll down and tap Reset progress (stars), then Reset.

This removes all stars. Your settings stay as they are.

Option 2: Clear all app data in Android

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Rhythm Workshop.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear storage (or Clear data).

This removes stars and settings. Menu names differ slightly between phones.

Option 3: Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Rhythm Workshop deletes everything it stored.

Questions or a written request

If you would still like us to confirm that we hold no information about you, email us with the subject "Rhythm Workshop data request". We'll reply within 30 days.
