Rhythm Workshop: Privacy Policy

Published by HomiLabs Solutions · Effective 25 September 2026

Rhythm Workshop collects no personal information and does not connect to the internet.

What the app stores

Only two things, and only on your device: the stars your child has earned, and the settings a parent chooses (voice language, volume, vibration, calm mode, session length and audio delay). This information never leaves the device. "Reset progress" in the app's parent settings deletes the stars; uninstalling the app deletes everything.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, names, photos, voice recording or location.
  • No advertising, no in-app purchases and no links out of the app.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send or receive data.

Children

The app is designed for children aged 3 to 6. Because it collects no personal information, it complies with COPPA, GDPR-K and the Google Play Families Policy by design. Parent settings sit behind a parental gate.

Changes

If a future version ever changed any of this, we would update this page and ask for parental consent in the app before collecting anything.
